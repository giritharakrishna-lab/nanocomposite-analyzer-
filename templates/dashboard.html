Governing computational models

Halpin–Tsai: $$E_L = E_m \frac{1 + \xi \eta_L V_f}{1 - \eta_L V_f}, \quad \eta_L = \frac{(E_{f,\mathrm{eff}}/E_m)-1}{(E_{f,\mathrm{eff}}/E_m)+\xi}$$

Kapitza resistance: $$k_{f,\mathrm{eff}} = \frac{k_f}{1 + 2 R_K k_f/L}, \quad k_{\mathrm{eff}} = k_m \frac{1 + p V_f (k_{f,\mathrm{eff}}-k_m)/k_m}{1 - V_f}$$

Electrical percolation: $$\sigma_{\mathrm{eff}} = \sigma_m + \sigma_f \eta_i (V_f - \phi_c)^t, \quad V_f > \phi_c$$
